2 Before doors open (4:45 PM)

  1. Charge your phone and turn the screen brightness up. Mobile data or venue Wi-Fi both work.
  2. Open Guest Check-in, type the PIN, and enter your station name, e.g. Main Entrance – Priya.
    The very first login of the day can take 10–20 seconds. That's normal.
  3. Do a test search, e.g. your own last name, to see results appear. Don't tap anyone yet.
  4. Leave the page open. You stay signed in until you tap Sign out.

3 Checking in a guest (about 5 seconds)

  1. Ask: "May I have the last 4 digits of your phone?" The name works too.
  2. Type it in the search box. Matches appear instantly.
  3. Tap the guest's name. The screen turns GREEN and shows their serial number in large text.
  4. Tell the guest their serial number and send them to the badge table: "You're number 27. Please pick up badge 27 at the next table. Your seat is on the badge. Happy Diwali!"
  5. The screen goes back to search by itself after 4 seconds. On to the next guest.

Tip: when only one guest matches, pressing Enter checks them in too.

4 If something's different

Screen turns amber ALREADY CHECKED IN
They (or someone with the same details) were already checked in. The screen shows when and at which station, plus their serial number. If they haven't collected their badge yet, send them to the badge table. If it looks wrong, send them to the help desk.
"No serial no."shown instead of a number
They're checked in, but have no badge number on the list. Send them to the help desk for a badge and seat.
Two people with the same name
Ask for the last 4 digits of their phone or their email and match it to the grey line under the name.
Guest not on the list
Try another spelling, their phone number, or their partner's name (couples often RSVP once). Still nothing? Tap + Walk-in, enter their name (phone if they'll share it), and tap Check in. Send them to the help desk for a badge and seat.
Badge table needs to look someone up
On the check-in page, type the serial number (e.g. 27) to see whose badge it is. Don't tap the name unless you mean to check them in.
Tapped the wrong person
On the green screen, tap Undo check-in straight away, then check in the right guest.
"Offline" or "waiting to sync"at the top
Keep checking guests in. They're saved on your phone and sent automatically when the connection returns. Don't sign out until it says Synced.
Google is responding slowly
Wait a few seconds and try again. Your saved check-ins are not lost.
"Wrong PIN"
Re-type the 6 digits carefully. If it still fails, ask your coordinator. Too many wrong tries locks it for 10 minutes.

5 Good habits

  • Greet first, then search: "Happy Diwali! Last 4 of your phone?"
  • Search by phone digits. It's the fastest and avoids spelling issues.
  • Keep the line moving. Send unusual cases to the help desk.
  • Tap Refresh if the coordinator says the guest list was updated.
  • Don't read guests' phone numbers or emails out loud.
  • Don't hand your phone to guests or leave it unlocked unattended.
  • Don't open the Attendance Report on a screen guests can see (it lists contact details).
  • Don't sign out while it says "waiting to sync".

6 At the end (8:00 PM)

  1. Check the top of your screen says Synced.
  2. Tap Sign out.
  3. Thank you! Every check-in is recorded automatically. No paperwork needed. 🪔
